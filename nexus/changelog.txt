v1.4.0 - 2026-10-23
Changed: Tend All replaces the game's own Tend Garden (game 1.1.19611). Set EnablePatrol to false to use the game's Tend Garden instead.
Removed: Support for game versions before 1.1.19611.

v1.3.0 - 2026-10-22
Added: Each floor and area line of the pot grid shows the light and the temperature of that area, the same values as the HUD, so you see which pots a cold wave hits.
Added: Pin the plant list to keep it open.
Changed: The pot grid groups the pots of each floor by area (Interior, Sunroom, Terrace, Cold Storage, Basement), with a small name line for each floor and each extra area. The floor name column is gone, and a row holds up to 7 pots, so the list is about as wide as Trapline's trap list.
Fixed: Better stability: Greenline no longer waits for answers from the game's web UI, a known cause of web UI crashes.

v1.2.0 - 2026-10-13
Added: Tend All walks the shortest route around walls and furniture: the floor you are on first, then the other floors.
Added: The planting window shows the pot's last crop next to its title, and in red why Auto-replant stopped, for example seeds 0/1.
Added: Same as last crop in the Sowing Presets of the Farming GR-3: the robot replants each pot with its last crop.
Added: The Farming GR-3 tills and plants a pot it harvested before its next harvest, also after it stops to charge, so the pot does not stay empty. EnableRobotReplant in the config file turns off both robot features.
Added: Tend All leaves a pot that a Farming GR-3 is on its way to, so your character and the robot never work on the same pot.
Changed: Auto-replant under the pot grid is on by default in each save. If you turn it off, it stays off.
Changed: The pot card shows next to its grid cell or above its pot, with an arrow, and follows the pot when the camera moves.
Changed: Better performance: the pot card in the game world updates only when you point at a different pot, not on every mouse move.
Changed: Tend All no longer depends on the game's hidden feature.
Removed: The game's Tend ×N quick action in the bottom bar.
Removed: The red reason box above the Plant button, which could push the button out of the window.
Removed: Support for game versions before 1.1.18599. Greenline 1.2.0 needs Survival Log 1.1.18599 or later.
Fixed: The pot grid shows the new language at once after you switch the display language.
Fixed: With a community language pack, Greenline shows its texts in English, or in Chinese for a Chinese pack.
Fixed: A pot grid countdown stays inside its cell, also in Chinese. From 10 days it shows the days only, for example 12d.

v1.1.0 - 2026-09-29
Added: Auto-fertilize also plants with a part dose when you have less fertilizer than the pot needs, as the game does since version 1.1.
Added: Overwinter No.1 shows Needs Fertilizer in the pot grid and on the pot card.
Added: The pot card shows the dose of a part dose after the fertilizer name, for example 1/2.
Changed: Auto-fertilize picks the fertilizer that gives the largest speed bonus, a part dose included. The message shows only when you have no fertilizer.
Changed: Pest or Frost on Overwinter No.1 shows as a stop of growth, not a threat, and a mature Overwinter No.1 shows no harvest countdown.
Removed: Support for game version 1.0. Greenline 1.1.0 needs Survival Log 1.1.18153 or later.

v1.0.1 - 2026-09-27
Fixed: Greenline reduces repeated work in the game's interface during play. The countdowns and the pot card update four times per second instead of every frame. The pot grid and the pot card keep the same appearance and controls.
