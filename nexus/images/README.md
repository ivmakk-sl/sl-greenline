# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images were prepared by hand from in-game screenshots. The wheat icon is the game's own icon of the HUD plant list (`Res/icon/icon_line_nature_18.png` of the game's web UI).

- `sl-greenline-banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the game's pot action buttons, a green vine, the pot grid, and the pot card.
- `sl-greenline-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): the "Green Line" wordmark and the wheat icon, beside the pot grid with the "Tend All" button and the pot card of a growing Tomato. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-greenline-auto.jpg` - the pot options (1920x1080): "Auto-replant & Auto-fertilize", with arrows to the global Auto-replant checkbox under the grid and to the two checkboxes of each pot, in the window of a growing crop and in the planting window.
- `sl-greenline-sample1.jpg` - a full in-game screenshot (1920x1080): the open pot grid in the HUD, with the card of a ripe Strawberry next to its cell.
- `sl-greenline-sample2.jpg` - a full in-game screenshot (1920x1080): the pot card next to the pointer on a ripe Strawberry pot in the game world, with the plant list closed.
